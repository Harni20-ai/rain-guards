Knowledge Base

Client Care FAQ

Immediate clarity on ordering protocols, customs documentation, and handcrafted timelines.

What is the typical timeline for a bespoke handle commission? expand_more

Bespoke handle commissions involving rare hardwoods, sterling silver fittings, or custom monograms require between 3 to 5 weeks of dedicated artisan crafting in our European and Japanese workshops prior to dispatch.

How are international customs and import duties handled? expand_more

All shipments dispatched from Aurumbrella are sent DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) for major regions including the EU, UK, US, and Japan. You will incur zero unexpected customs fees upon delivery.

What does the lifetime structural warranty cover? expand_more

Our lifetime warranty covers all structural components including the aerospace-grade shaft, fiberglass rib assemblies, and runner mechanisms. Accidental fabric tears or surface cosmetic scratches can be serviced through our atelier refurbishment program.

Can I request a private virtual consultation? expand_more

Yes. Private video consultations with our senior vault curators can be scheduled via our concierge desk to examine canopy swatches, handle grains, and custom engraving options in real-time.
